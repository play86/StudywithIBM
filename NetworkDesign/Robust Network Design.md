# 🌐 Network Design Principles

> Robust network design rests on three foundational pillars.

## 🎯 Core Principles

| Principle | Focus |
|---|---|
| 🔄 **Resilience** | Keeping the network running through failures |
| 🛡️ **Security** | Protecting against threats and intrusions |
| 📈 **Scalability** | Growing sustainably with demand |

---

## 🔄 Design for Resilience

We consider, under this design:

- ✅ Self-healing
- ✅ Failover systems
- ✅ Load balancing
- ✅ Automatic monitoring
- ✅ Redundancy

> 🔒 **N.B. Hardening:** use of IDS' (Intrusion Detection Systems) and firewalls to further safeguard and resist attacks, snooping, or intrusion.

---

## 🛡️ Design for Security

We group the considerations into:

### 🖥️ A. Threats to Hardware
- Physical attacks
- Firmware attacks
- Device spoofing

### 📡 B. Threats to Network Traffic
| Threat | Type |
|---|---|
| **DOS** | Denial of Service attacks |
| **DDOS** | Distributed Denial of Service attacks |
| **Eavesdropping** | Attacks |
| **Hijacking** | Attacks |
